Introducing the stunning 2026 GMC Sierra 3500HD AT4 4WD Crew Cab Standard Box, a powerful and stylish truck for those who demand both performance and luxury. Dressed in an eye-catching black exterior complemented by a refined black interior, this 4×4 beast is engineered for the toughest challenges. With its robust Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine, delivering a mighty 470 horsepower and 975 lb-ft of torque, and paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, the Sierra 3500HD AT4 promises a commanding drive on any terrain.

On the outside, the high gloss black aluminum wheels and all-terrain blackwall tires add a touch of rugged elegance to its imposing stance. The power sunroof and power-retractable assist steps with LED lighting ensure both style and practicality whether you’re tackling the great outdoors or cruising through the city.

Inside, immerse yourself in luxury with front perforated leather seats with Kalahari accents that offer both heating and cooling features. The advanced 13.4-inch diagonal Premium GMC Infotainment System, complete with Google built-in apps such as navigation, wireless Android Auto, and Apple CarPlay, ensures you stay connected wherever the road takes you. The technology package further enhances your journey with a multicolor 15-inch diagonal Head-Up Display and a rear camera mirror that provides an extended view.

Safety and intelligent convenience are top priorities with features like adaptive cruise control, lane assist, parking sensors, and a smart device integration. The AT4 comes equipped with GMC's Trailer Tire Pressure Monitor Sensors to assist in safe towing and the Universal Vehicle Module for integrative vehicle management.
